The space

Just a couple of steps (literally) from the beach

Take a long walk on the beach in the sunset or early in the sunrise, teach your little ones to swim, or just play with them in the water right under the house's garden, take a swim anytime of the day, or just relax under the pine trees by reading or simply taking an afternoon siesta, listening to the relaxing sea waves and enjoy that refreshing sea breeze feeling.

This unique beach front private house is situated in a lush green environment, with a beautiful garden and a fantastic sea view. It is ideal for family vacation, calmness, tranquility and relaxation. The home is furnished with treasured handmade pieces and the exterior is clad in timber panelling.

Unlike similar properties at the area, what makes this property stand out, is that it is privately fenced and you do not get to share the garden and yard with anyone else. Most importantly, there is direct and private access to the beach from the garden by the stone stairs.
